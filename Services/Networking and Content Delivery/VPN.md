Connect your on-premises network to [[AWS]]
Uses [[IPsec]] to authenticate and encrypt
Made up of two services:
- [[Site-to-Site VPN]]
	- Create encrypted tunnel between on-site environment to Site-to-Site endpoint which connects to Virtual Private Gateway
- [[Client VPN]]
	- Create encrypted tunnel between public internet and Client [[VPC]] Endpoint
