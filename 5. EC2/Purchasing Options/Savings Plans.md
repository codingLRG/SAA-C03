Flexible pricing model for [[EC2]], [[Lambda]] and [[Fargate]]
Discounts in exchange for commitment to a consistent usage amount
- $perhr for 1 or 3 year term
Purchased from:
- [[AWS]] account
- Management account of [[AWS]] org
- Member account of [[AWS]] org


| Savings plans                                                               | [[Reserved Instances]]                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Based on consistent amount of compute usage                                 | Based on specific instance type or size                       |
| Flexibility to use more suitable compute without exchanged or modifications | Must exchange or modify instance to suit current requirements |
