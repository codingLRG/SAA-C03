[[SageMaker]]
AI ethics and explainability toolkit.
Transparent, fair ML models

Bias Detection:
- Pre-training bias: Training data
- Post-training bias: Output data
- Fairness metrics: Approval rates across different groups

Model Explainability:
- Global explanations: What mattered most for output
- Local explanations: Why specific output
- Partial dependence plots: How changing one feature affect predictions

Explainability Methods:
- SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP): Shows feature contribution to prediction
- Feature Attribution: Quantifies impact
- Contractual Explanations: Shows how different features change prediction
