Samba 4 AD compatible server
Comes in sizes small and large
Features:
- Manage user accounts and group memberships
- Creating and applying GPs
- Secure connect [[EC2]]
- [[Kerberos]] [[SSO]]

Lacks:
- Trust relationships with other domains
- MFA
- Schema extensions
- Group-managed service accounts
- PowerShell support
- AD Admin Center
- AD Recycle Bi
